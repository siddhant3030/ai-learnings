# NE India Context: CSR Spend Verification, Funder Landscape, Node/SeSTA/FFRH

> Research agent report, 7 Aug 2026. Part of the FFRH funder-intelligence landscape scan — see [README.md](README.md).

## 1. CSR spend disparity — claim verification

**The claim ("NE gets 3-4% vs 17% for Maharashtra") is broadly correct but understated: the NE receives ~2-3%, and Maharashtra's share has grown past 17% to ~21% in the latest year.**

**FY2024-25 (latest, MCA National CSR Portal data, presented in Parliament by FM Nirmala Sitharaman):**
- National CSR total: **Rs 40,794 crore** (up 17% YoY)
- Maharashtra: **Rs 8,630.80 crore (~21.2%)**; Gujarat Rs 4,549.10 cr; Karnataka Rs 3,395.43 cr
- NE state-wise: Assam **Rs 607.04 cr**, Meghalaya 62.07, Sikkim 54.55, Arunachal 40.06, Manipur 33.73, Nagaland 26.79, Tripura 15.89, Mizoram 14.97 — **NE total ≈ Rs 855 crore ≈ 2.1%** of national spend, and Assam alone is 71% of that
- Sources: [MorungExpress, citing csr.gov.in / Parliament reply of 3 Aug 2026](https://morungexpress.com/csr-spending-in-nagaland-hits-record-rs-2679-crore-in-fy202425); [IndiaCSR FY25 record piece](https://indiacsr.in/indias-csr-hits-record-crore-in-fy-2024-25/)

**FY2023-24:** national total Rs 34,908.75 cr; Maharashtra Rs 6,066 cr (~17.4%); every NE state except Assam under Rs 85 cr; top 6 states took >60% ([IndiaCSR](https://indiacsr.in/csr-spending-in-india-reaches-rs-34909-crore-in-fy24-report/); [Deccan Herald study coverage](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2F60-of-csr-funds-go-to-top-6-states-new-study-points-to-unequal-spending-3672141)).

**Caveat for the proposal:** csr.gov.in state-wise figures exclude large "PAN-India" allocations, so exact percentages shift by methodology — safe phrasing is "the eight NE states together receive roughly 2% of India's CSR spend (Rs ~855 cr of Rs 40,794 cr, FY2024-25), versus over 21% for Maharashtra alone." FFRH's own page uses a related stat: "Only 3% of national philanthropic funding reaches the North East."

## 2. Funder landscape

**Confirmed NoDe/SeSTA supporters (listed on node.sesta.org):** Axis Bank Foundation (NoDe's anchor supporter), Azim Premji Foundation, Tata Trusts, Dasra, Forbes Marshall.

- **Azim Premji Foundation** — grants programme explicitly prioritises **Northeast India** (and North Karnataka) for early-stage CSOs; multi-year grants across education, health, livelihoods, gender ([apply page](https://azimpremjifoundation.org/apply-for-a-grant/); [NGOBox announcement](https://ngobox.org/full_grant_announcement_Applications-Invited-for-Azim-Premji-Foundation-Grants-for-Early-stage-CSOs-_12203))
- **Tata Trusts** — long NE presence; created its own implementation vehicles (NEIDA, Kohima) after finding local NGO partner capacity a constraint ([Tata Trusts Horizons](https://horizons.tatatrusts.org/2019/march/tata-trusts-northeast-social-development.html))
- **Hans Foundation** — active recent commitment: partnership with Govt of Assam for "Uttam Xikhya" (100 tea-garden schools, 5 years) plus 24 Mobile Medical Units across 120 tea gardens in Dibrugarh/Tinsukia ([BW Wellbeing World](https://www.bwwellbeingworld.com/article/the-hans-foundation-partners-with-assam-government-526446))
- **HDFC Parivartan** — pan-India ECSS/scholarship and rural development programmes; no NE-specific commitment surfaced in searches
- **PSU CSR (Oil India, NRL, ONGC, NEDFi)** — no specific 2024-26 CSR-commitment figures surfaced via search; these need direct annual-report pulls (their CSR is structurally NE-concentrated given operations). Nagaland's record Rs 26.79 cr FY25 CSR is attributed partly to PSU inflows.

**Government/macro tailwind (usable framing):** the **Rising Northeast Investors Summit (New Delhi, 23-24 May 2025)** drew **Rs 4.3 lakh crore in investment commitments** — Reliance Rs 75,000 cr/5 yrs, Adani Rs 50,000 cr/10 yrs, Vedanta Rs 30,000+ cr ([PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2131072); [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/rising-northeast-investors-summit-2025-attracts-investment-commitments-of-4-3-lakh-crore/amp)). As these investments localise, Section 135 CSR obligations will follow into NE states — a strong argument that CSO fundraising-readiness is time-critical. MDoNER continues PM-DevINE (Rs 6,600 cr, 2022-26) and NESIDS/NEC schemes ([Shillong Times, Sep 2025](https://theshillongtimes.com/2025/09/13/act-east-centre-implements-series-of-transformative-initiatives-to-boost-inclusive-growth/)).

## 3. Node/SeSTA and FFRH (fetched Aug 2026)

- **SeSTA** (Seven Sisters Development Assistance, est. 2011, Assam) works on rural livelihoods, women in farming, community institutions across NE states ([sesta.org](https://sesta.org/)).
- **NoDe** ([node.sesta.org](https://node.sesta.org/)) is SeSTA's CSO capacity-building initiative (anchor: Axis Bank Foundation): leadership, governance/systems, people & culture, fundraising & communication, learning/collaboration; runs four cohorts plus research and a resource repository. Tagline: "Strong CSOs make strong communities — and Northeast India deserves both."
- **FFRH** ([node.sesta.org/ffrh/](https://node.sesta.org/ffrh/)) — "a time-bound capacity-building platform designed for high-potential CSOs in Northeast India," incubated within SeSTA. Model: **shared/fractional fundraising expertise** over a **three-year engagement cycle**; two pillars — (a) CSO readiness (programme articulation, evidence, compliance, fundraising planning) and (b) funder engagement (curating CSO pipelines, aligning with funder priorities, matchmaking). Entry via a Capacity Assessment (programme clarity, governance, financial compliance, documentation, funder engagement). Goal: **100 CSOs by 2035**. Contact: node@sesta.org.

**Implication:** FFRH already does readiness + matchmaking; a fundraising-intelligence layer (data on funders, CSR flows, pipeline tracking) is complementary, not duplicative.

## 4. Adjacent intermediaries

- **Foundation for Social Transformation (FST), Guwahati** — indigenous NE philanthropy (est. 2005), grant-making **plus** CSO capacity building across all seven sister states; closest analogue/partner ([fstindia.org](https://fstindia.org/in/))
- **NEIDA (North East Initiative Development Agency), Kohima** — Tata Trusts' NE implementation arm ([Tata Trusts partners](https://www.tatatrusts.org/list-of-partners))
- **NEDFi** — DFI with CSR and NGO-partnering role in NE (specific CSR figures need direct annual-report lookup)
- **Dasra** — listed as a NoDe supporter, so already engaged in NE CSO strengthening
- **CSRBOX / India CSR Outlook Report** — data source; profiles SeSTA and NE CSR flows ([ICOR 2025](https://csrboximpact.in/storage/publications/Official%20ICOR%20Report%202025_Publication_Website.pdf))
