# Fundraising-Intelligence Market: DaanVeda vs. Sattva FundAssist

> Research agent report, 7 Aug 2026. Part of the FFRH funder-intelligence landscape scan — see [README.md](README.md).

## 1. DaanVeda ([daanveda.com](https://www.daanveda.com/))

**What it is.** A self-serve, product-led "Unified Fundraising OS" for Indian nonprofits. Founded May 2024 by Irfan Bashir (CEO), Tushar Nautiyal, Sneha Singhal; HQ Noida ([Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/daanveda/___gqDmxeY4RG0p6QWheg8jp7_TZtsFOFwQ_t2s5q3vjg), [Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/daanveda)).

**Database claims** (homepage + [pricing page](https://www.daanveda.com/pricing)): 40K–52K Indian CSR companies with budgets/sector focus/giving history; ~413K foundation profiles (global); 195K+ RFPs/grants refreshed daily; 5M+ decision-makers; ~2M philanthropists/UHNIs; a "200M+ HNI" dataset on the Enterprise tier. These are marketing figures — no independent verification found.

**AI features.** "Veda AI"/FundraiserGPT: AI proposal drafting grounded in an indexed "Knowledge Hub" of the org's documents; AI donor–opportunity matching with relevancy scores; email enrichment, sentiment analysis, automated multi-step outreach journeys; a CRM-spreadsheet hybrid ("Unisheets"). Plus DaanVeda Academy (training, with Bhumi and Ketto Foundation).

**Pricing.** Freemium: free-forever tier (10 grants/month, 10 CSR/foundation profiles, Academy); Premium ₹1,00,000/yr + GST (1,800 credits/mo, 3 seats); Enterprise ₹2,00,000/yr + GST (6,000 credits/mo, 20 seats); 25–65% grassroots-NGO discounts. Credit-based unlocking of contacts and AI chats. **No API access or partner program is mentioned anywhere on the pricing/site.**

**Traction.** Claims 800+ nonprofits, ₹312 Cr donor commitments tracked in 2025; named users include Educate Girls, Bhumi, Ketto Foundation. Very small company: ~27–28 employees (mid-2026), only ~$64K–110K raised (seed, Jul 2025) ([Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/daanveda/___gqDmxeY4RG0p6QWheg8jp7_TZtsFOFwQ_t2s5q3vjg), [Inc42](https://inc42.com/company/daanveda/)).

**North East India:** no NE-specific coverage or specialization found. **Open source / data sharing:** nothing found — proprietary, contact-credit monetization suggests a closed-data posture.

## 2. Sattva Consulting — FundAssist ([sattva.co.in/our-work/non-profits/fundassist](https://www.sattva.co.in/our-work/non-profits/fundassist/))

**What it is.** A data-plus-advisory offering from Sattva (impact consulting firm, Bengaluru, founded 2009, ~500+ corporate/funder relationships). FundAssist bundles a **1-year custom data dashboard subscription** with curated directories and ongoing helpdesk support — advisory-led, not pure SaaS.

**Database.** 37,000+ companies/CSR funders (priorities, spending patterns, preferred funding models) and 13,000+ affluent individuals; peer-funding intelligence (which funders fund similar NGOs) and decision-maker contacts.

**AI features.** None advertised — it is a curated-data/insight product, not an AI platform. No matching engine, proposal drafting, or donor-comms automation mentioned.

**Pricing.** Not published; demo/contact-driven (fundassist@sattva.co.in). One third-party report puts it around **~$2,000** (~₹1.7L) — treat as unverified. No free tier, no API mentioned.

**Traction.** No named FundAssist clients or case studies published; it sits inside Sattva's broader nonprofit advisory practice (fundraising strategy, org development). Sattva itself is large and established but FundAssist-specific traction is not disclosed.

**North East India:** no NE-specific coverage found; India-wide framing. **Data-sharing posture:** notably open elsewhere — Sattva runs [India Data Insights](https://indiadatainsights.com/), a free open portal of SDG/development-capital data, and publishes via its Knowledge Institute; FundAssist data itself is paid/closed. Sattva also operates Shift ([shiftimpact.co.in](https://www.shiftimpact.co.in/)), a CSR-management SaaS — evidence they do productize and platform-partner.

## Build-vs-Partner Assessment

**DaanVeda — partner-plausible, but fragile.** It has already built exactly the stack you'd otherwise build (CSR/RFP database + AI matching + proposal drafting), is cheap (₹1–2L/yr, deep NGO discounts), and is hungry (27 people, <$150K raised) — a startup that size would likely welcome a distribution/reseller or data-integration partnership even though no API or partner program exists publicly today. Risks: unverified database quality, closed data, credit-metering that fights bulk/programmatic use, and survival risk given minimal funding. A pilot partnership (bulk NGO licensing, co-branded access) is low-cost to test before committing to build.

**FundAssist — partner for credibility and funder relationships, not for tech.** It is an advisory wrapper around a curated dataset with no AI layer, so it doesn't substitute for a product you'd build — but Sattva's 500+ corporate/funder network, open-data track record (India Data Insights), and existing platform business (Shift) make them a natural data or go-to-market partner rather than a competitor. The realistic partnership is data licensing or joint offerings for NGO cohorts; expect consulting-style pricing and no self-serve API. If North East India specialization matters, neither platform offers it — that gap is buildable regardless of which partnership you pursue.

**Gaps explicitly not found:** DaanVeda API/white-label terms, independent verification of either database's accuracy, FundAssist official pricing, and any NE-India coverage claims from either vendor.
